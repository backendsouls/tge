# Power System: Macross (Song Energy & Fold Waves)

## Overview
The *Macross* franchise bridges hard sci-fi mecha combat with pop idol culture. The power system literally relies on the power of music, which over the decades of the franchise evolved from a cultural metaphor into a strictly quantified physics phenomenon called "Fold Waves."

## Core Mechanics
1. **Fold Waves**
   Fold waves are naturally occurring energy ripples in super-dimensional space, primarily used for Faster-Than-Light (FTL) travel and communication.

2. **Song Energy**
   Certain biologically gifted humans (or aliens) can naturally project their emotions and consciousness as Fold Waves through the act of singing. Because these waves travel through super-dimensional space, they can bypass physical barriers, vacuums, and distance.

3. **Combat Buffs and Pacification**
   - **Pacification**: The fold waves generated by song resonate with the neural frequencies of alien hive-minds (like the Zentradi or Vajra), overriding their combat intent and pacifying them.
   - **Mecha Enhancement**: In modern series (like *Macross Delta*), Tactical Sound Units use "Fold Resonance." The singer's energy is routed directly into the engines of allied Valkyrie fighter jets, massively boosting their speed, shielding, and weapon output (similar to a Bard buff in RPGs, but quantified by physics).

## Simulation / Implementation Concepts for CLI
- **Aura / AoE Buffing**: Introduce a `BardicState` or `SongEnergy` action. When activated, it doesn't deal damage but applies a continuous `+30% Evasion/Damage` modifier to all allied entities in the simulation array for as long as the "Song" action is maintained.
- **Mental Override**: Against specific enemy archetypes (e.g., `HiveMind = true`), a `Song` action triggers a `Willpower` check that can force the enemy to swap their alignment to neutral, instantly ending combat without HP depletion.
